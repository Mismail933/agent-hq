# Bassel's map channel: style guide

Every point here comes from Bassel or the owner, on Chapter 1 and its Short (approved 2026-10-31). Read it before any new chapter. Project 6.
No Israa or Tarek rounds for this channel. Run this list yourself before rendering, on stills of every change.

## How we work with Bassel
- No work on a part until he has sent his remarks and said go.
- Long version first, then the Short, when he asks for both.
- Everything sent to him also goes to the owner's phone.
- His approved words and approved scenes stay. A new version starts from the approved one, never from a rejected one.
- If a thing he asked for doesn't fit, say so plainly when you send the video.

## Look
- One look for the whole video: the real Earth (NASA Blue Marble) with white borders and solid coloured countries. Never a second map style for a surface: New France is drawn on the same map as everything else.
- The war-table scenes are for events (a fleet, a settlement), never to show the extent of a territory.
- Never the Storygraphics cast or kit.
- Readable names, large enough for a phone, inside the frame, clear of one another. Move a label rather than let it touch another.
- Regions that touch share the exact same border points. A colony never overlaps its neighbour.
- No title bands or section cards across the middle.

## The camera
- It moves only when the story moves, slowly, like a person would. No spinning left and right, no zooming in and out for its own sake.
- A long flight doesn't shrink the globe and regrow it. A country only named doesn't get a flight: a small corner flag, the camera stays.
- A place tour on the globe: hold on the first area until its first name is said, then turn at an even speed, and stop when the last places named are all in one frame.
- To end a chapter on a place, zoom slowly onto it on the closing line.

## Lit points
- A lit point means France holds it at that moment. It goes out when the story loses it. Places not yet reached aren't lit.

## Pictures and clips
- A picture only where it says what the line says, from the right period. No random pictures.
- Portraits sit beside the map, the map stays visible. Two people at once: one each side.
- A picture stays long enough to look at (about 4 s), and soft fades in and out, never a sudden cut or a circle. Paintings and engravings may fill the screen.
- Moving AI clips are labelled as recreations, never "real footage".
- Downloads: public domain, CC0 or CC BY only, checked for each file.

## Words and voice
- Don't say what's already been said (Bourbon = Reunion once). Dates only when they matter (no list of trading-post dates).
- A real full stop between paragraphs that change subject, with a pause you can feel.
- Voice: Chris (ElevenLabs `iP95p4xoKVk53GoZ742B`) for finished videos, on the owner's plan. Natural pace, no per-line tempo. Script `voice: "eleven:<id>"`, `story_gap` 0.6.
- Sound: real recordings only, effects at least 15 dB under the voice, never over it.

## Before sending
- A frame every 2 s of the whole video, read against his list, plus a look for anything else wrong.
- Measure length, loudness and effects against the voice. Say what was measured and that the sound wasn't heard.
- Check free disk space before a long render; render in pieces from one bundle.
